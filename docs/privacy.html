🌙 Moonlight

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Moonlight may process while operating in Discord servers.

Data We May Store

Moonlight may store limited Discord-related identifiers needed for bot features, such as user IDs, server IDs, pet progress, inventory items, command-related settings, moderation notes, or similar gameplay and utility data.

Data We Do Not Store

Moonlight does not intentionally store passwords, payment information, private Discord tokens, or full message history. Some commands may temporarily read message content only when needed to perform the requested action.

How Data Is Used

Stored data is used only to provide Moonlight features, keep progress, support moderation tools, and improve the bot experience.

Data Sharing

Moonlight does not sell user data. Data is not shared with third parties except where required for hosting, security, maintenance, or legal compliance.

Data Removal

Server owners or users may request removal of stored bot data by contacting the bot owner.

Changes

This policy may be updated over time. Continued use of Moonlight means you accept the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions or removal requests, contact the bot owner through the Discord server or repository where Moonlight is maintained.
